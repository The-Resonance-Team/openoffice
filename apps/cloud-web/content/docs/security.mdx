## Your documents stay yours

We never train on your files or data. Session history is retained only to render your timeline and can be deleted at any time.

## Credentials

Provider keys are stored through the Cred Proxy and are never exposed to the client.

## Data location

You control whether models hosted in specific regions are enabled for routing.
