## Google Workspace

Connect Drive, Docs, Sheets and Gmail so the agent can open files and draft replies for your approval.

## Microsoft 365

Link SharePoint, Word, Excel and Outlook with one click from the org console.

## CRM & more

Add Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack and DocuSign as integrations, scoped to org or team.

## Scopes & permissions

Every integration is granted per workspace or org-wide, and can be revoked at any time from Settings.
